Raze Store

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026 · Android package: com.remyo.razestore

This policy explains how Raze Store, published by remyo, handles information when you use the app. Raze Store provides product price lookup, checkout calculations, and store recordkeeping tools. You do not need to create an account or sign in.

Store information kept on your device

The app stores information you enter in its local database and files. This can include your store name and receipt contact details; product names, barcodes, prices and photos; sales and receipts; customer names, contact details, store-credit (utang) balances and repayments; manually recorded GCash transaction details and receipt images; cashbook entries, expenses, budgets, and cash counts.

These records support the features you choose to use. Raze Store does not automatically upload your store records or photos to a developer-operated server. We do not operate an online account or cloud-sync service for this version of the app.

Camera, photos, and text recognition

The camera is used when you scan barcodes or capture product and receipt images. You may also select images or files through your device's picker. Barcode recognition, text recognition, photo optimization, and background removal process the selected content on the device. Recognized text is presented for you to review.

The app does not upload the images or recognized text to the developer for recognition. Technical diagnostics from Google's ML Kit are described separately below.

Technical data collected by Google ML Kit

Raze Store uses Google ML Kit for barcode scanning and text recognition. According to Google's SDK disclosure, ML Kit can send technical information to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics. This includes:

  • Device model, manufacturer, operating-system version, and available machine-learning hardware.
  • App package name, app version, and per-installation identifiers.
  • Feature-use events, configuration, feature versions, input/output sizes, latency, and error codes.

This technical data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Raze Store does not provide a switch to disable the SDK's diagnostic collection. Google states that ML Kit does not transfer the listed data to third parties. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy for details about Google's processing and retention. We do not claim a fixed retention period for data managed by Google.

Backups, exports, and sharing you choose

You can export backups, catalogs, CSV files, and receipts, or share them through other apps. Full backup files may contain customer details, transaction records, and attached images. The location or app you select receives those files and handles them under its own privacy practices. Copies outside Raze Store remain until you delete them there.

If you use local-network sharing, selected product details and images are sent to the device you pair with and approve. Transfers use authenticated encryption. This feature does not send your sales, customer, utang, GCash, expense, or store-settings records. Your operating system may also back up or restore app data according to your device and backup-provider settings.

Permissions and notifications

Camera access enables scanning and photography. Image and file access enables selection, import, export, and storage. Local-network access supports transfers you initiate. Optional notification permission allows local reminders. You can manage permissions and notifications in your device settings; disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

Financial records and advertising

GCash, wallet, and utang tools are manual recordkeeping features. Raze Store does not hold money, connect to financial accounts, verify external payments, issue loans, or transfer funds. Do not enter account passwords or one-time security codes into records or share them with support.

This version does not display advertising or use the Android advertising identifier. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local records remain until you delete them using the relevant app controls or clear the app's storage. Uninstalling normally removes app-private storage, although device backups and exported copies may remain. Delete those copies separately through the service or device where they are stored. Clearing storage or uninstalling can permanently remove records that have not been backed up.

Because we do not receive your local store database, we cannot retrieve or delete those records remotely. SDK data handled by Google is governed by Google's policies and available controls. We cannot identify an individual user's ML Kit telemetry solely from a support email.

Support communications

If you contact support, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use them to respond and investigate your request. Send only the information needed for support, and remove customer or financial details from screenshots whenever possible. You may request deletion of support correspondence, subject to records we need to keep for unresolved requests or applicable legal obligations.

Younger users

Raze Store is intended for people aged 13 and older who run or help with store operations. It is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to support, contact us so we can review and address the request.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
